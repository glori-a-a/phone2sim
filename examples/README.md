# Recorded manipulation example

A personally recorded pushing demonstration from `phone_02`, a training recording. The real footage shows a handheld blue circular tool pushing the red object; it is separate from the MuJoCo simulation videos.

- [Recorded table view](recorded_push.mp4)
- [The same recording with tracking overlay](tracking_push.mp4)
- [Source hashes and processing details](provenance.json)

The measured 40 × 30 cm table quadrilateral is rectified and cropped at the tracked 10 Hz timestamps. Playback timing is preserved and audio is omitted. The original camera view, including room background, is retained privately. These clips are derived visual examples, not unmodified original recordings. The overlay adds marker centres and trajectories to the same frames.
